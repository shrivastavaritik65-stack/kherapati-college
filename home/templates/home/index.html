About Our College

A Place Where Education Meets Opportunity

Kherapati Sarkar Group Of Colleges, Indergarh (Datia), is a prominent educational institution committed to providing quality and value-based higher education to students from rural and semi-urban areas.

With modern infrastructure, experienced faculty and a rich academic environment, the institution continuously works towards the overall development of students and the creation of a bright future for the next generation.
