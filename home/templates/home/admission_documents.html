Admission Documents

Find the required documents for new UG/PG admission and the Village Girl scheme. Download the complete document lists as PDF.

UG / PG New Admission

Documents required for undergraduate and postgraduate admission.

  1. 10th Standard Mark Sheet
  2. 12th Standard Mark Sheet
  3. Graduation Mark Sheet
    Required for postgraduate admission
  4. Caste Certificate
  5. Domicile / Residence Certificate
  6. Income Certificate
  7. Samagra ID
  8. Photocopy of Aadhaar Card
  9. One Passport-size Photograph
  10. ABC ID
    Academic Bank of Credits ID

Village Girl Scheme

Documents required for eligible Village Girl applicants.

  1. Five-page form for first-year Village Girl applicants
  2. Completed online application form
  3. Photocopy of 10th Standard Mark Sheet
  4. Photocopy of 12th Standard Mark Sheet
  5. Online Fee Receipt
  6. Income Certificate
  7. Caste Certificate
  8. Domicile / Residence Certificate
  9. Family ID
  10. Aadhaar Card
  11. Bank Passbook
  12. Transfer Certificate (TC), as applicable
